Where every player becomes a star!

Social network for young hockey players, coaches and scouts with interactive interface and hockey theme.

For whom?

For players:

Make yourself known, become visible to scouts, ask for gifts from stars, find friends all over the hockey world!

For coaches:

Motivate your team, give assignments and track exercise completion and preparation for standards.

For scouts:

Select promising players from around the world, watch video compilations and contact directly.

For hockey workshops and stores:

Make yourself known, tell about your services, stimulate sales by providing discounts.

Main Features

Player Profiles

Create detailed profiles with information about position, statistics, teams and achievements

Messages

Communicate with other players, coaches and scouts in a convenient messenger

Speed Radar

Measure puck speed with radar and set records

Friends

Add friends, follow their achievements and communicate

Exercises

Complete exercises, track progress and earn achievements

Notifications

Receive notifications about new messages, friends and achievements
